Privacy — Samsung & LG TV edition

Playlists, search text and any credentials inside a playlist URL stay in memory for this app session. They are not saved to app storage or uploaded to an NM3 server. Closing the app clears its session; a media provider may retain its own records.

Loading a playlist or playing a stream connects directly to the host you choose. That host receives your IP address, network request information and any credentials in the source URL. Its privacy policy and retention rules apply. Optional technical samples connect to Google Cloud Storage or Apple's streaming host only when you play them. You can use your own sources instead.

This TV edition contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK or cloud account service. Use HTTPS sources to protect data in transit; HTTP sources are unencrypted.
