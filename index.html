A few useful answers

Before you press play.

Does the app include channels or movies?

No. NM3 StreamX is a player. You must supply sources you are legally authorized to access. Optional neutral technical clips may be available for playback testing.

Will every stream work on every device?

No. Playback depends on provider access, network conditions, codecs, DRM and hardware support. A format that works on one TV may not work on another device.

Can I use my streaming-service subscription?

A subscription alone does not authorize third-party player access. Use only sources your service explicitly permits. NM3 StreamX is not affiliated with streaming-service providers.

What should I include in a support request?

Send your app version, device model, operating-system version and the steps that caused the issue. Do not send passwords, PINs, private playlist URLs or identity documents.

Need a hand with your player?

Ask a question or report a playback or navigation issue.
